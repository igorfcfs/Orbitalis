# ORBITALIS: AI Use Declaration (draft)

NASA Space Apps requires teams to declare where and how AI was used. This text is for the submission and for the README. Items marked **CONFIRM** are things only the team can verify.

## AI in the running product

- One optional feature: a short explanation of why a system has its status and which catalog action fits (RF-18). The model is `claude-haiku-5-5` (Anthropic), called only from a server route.
- The AI does **not** compute, alter or suggest a status. Status is computed by deterministic code and tested in `src/engine/status.test.ts`.
- The AI receives only numbers already on screen and may only pick actions by ID from a closed catalog. The server discards any answer that names an action outside the allowed list or contains a number that was not in the input. Temperature 0.2, 8 s timeout.
- With no key, on failure or on timeout, the app shows fixed fallback text marked `Offline explanation`. The app is complete without AI.
- No model was trained by the team.

## AI in development

- The code was written with the help of Claude Code (Anthropic, Claude Sonnet), under the direction and review of the team. **CONFIRM** that the team reviewed and takes responsibility for all merged code.
- Claude was also used for competition research, requirements drafting and documentation.
- The closed text catalogs (emergency steps) and the pre-flight profile notes were drafted with AI assistance and cite published sources. **CONFIRM** who checked each citation. Emergency protocols have not been validated by a physician.

## AI-generated media

- **CONFIRM:** no image, video, voice or music in the app or the demo was generated by AI. If any was, it must be labelled and watermarked per the competition rules.
- The heads, suit, maps and textures are third-party assets with their own licenses (see README).
